04 / KHAMOSHI

What
silence remembers.

Some words remain unspoken, but silence remembers every one of them.

01

Khamosh-e-dil ko samjhayein kaise,
Woh chali gayi mera pyaar thukra ke.
Gale lagane ki tamanna toh aaj bhi hai,
Magar uski khamoshi roz todti hai mujhe,
Aur hum chup-chaap bikharte rahe,
Usse apna samajh kar bhi use apna na bana sake.

02

Likha tha tere liye sab kuch,
Magar sunne ke liye tu hi nahi bachi.
Likhna toh aaj bhi chhod nahi sakta,
Tune mere lafzon ke saath mera pyaar bhi jala diya.

Kaash keh hi deta jo dil mein daba rakha tha,
Aaj tere jaane ka gham nahi,
Apni khamoshi ka afsos hota hai.

03

Zurm kya tha mera, jo yun khamoshi mein chhod ke chali gayi?
Khud apne haathon se maar deti mujhe, toh gila na hota.

Magar pehle ishq ka nasha chadhaya,
Phir beech safar mein tanha chhod diya.

Ab har saans tera diya hua dard lagti hai,
Aur har dhadkan poochti hai —
mera kasoor kya tha, jo mujhe itna tod ke chali gayi?

04

Kya khata thi meri, jo yun be-dard chhod ke chali gayi?
Khud keh deti ki mohabbat nahi, toh gila na hota.

Magar pehle apna banaya,
Phir gairon ki tarah tod ke chali gayi.

Ab dil bhi tujhe bhoolna chahta hai,
Aur yaadein tujhe jaane nahi deti.

05

Kya kami thi mujh mein, jo yun tanha chhod ke chali gayi?
Khud bata deti ki tera ishq mera tha hi nahi, toh dard na hota.

Magar umeed bhi tune di,
Aur wahi umeed tod ke chali gayi.

Ab mohabbat se bhi darr lagta hai,
Kyunki ek baar dil diya tha,
aur woh bhi haar gaye.
