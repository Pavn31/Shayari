02 / ISHQ

Things
called love.

Some feelings don't need a name. They simply become someone's name.

01

Tera zikr aaya toh dil ne
phir se dhadakna seekh liya,
warna humne toh kab ka
khud ko khamosh kar diya tha.

02

Tujhse mohabbat kuch yun hui,
ki tujhe paana zaroori na raha,
bas tera khayal hi kaafi tha,
aur dil ko koi shikayat na raha.

03

Woh saamne ho toh lafz kho jaate hain,
door ho toh khayal uske ho jaate hain,
ajeeb hai ye ishq bhi janaab,
hum khud se zyada uske ho jaate hain.

04

Tere naam se shuru,
tere naam par khatam hoti hai meri har baat,
tu samjhe ya na samjhe,
tu hi toh hai meri har raat.

05

Mohabbat mein hisaab kahan hota hai,
jo dil mein ho woh kitaab kahan hota hai,
tu mil jaaye toh mukammal ho jaaye kahaani,
warna ishq ka koi jawaab kahan hota hai.
