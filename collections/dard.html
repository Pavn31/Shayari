01 / DARD

Words
from pain.

Some wounds never speak. They become words instead.

01

Usne khwaab dikhaye the mehelon ke mujhe,
aur aaj uski yaadon ka pata shamshaan nikla.

02

Wo mohabbat ki baatein karta raha chand sitaaron tak,
aur main uske baad raat bhar apni qabr sa dil liye baitha raha.

03

Ishq-o-mohabbat hi dard ka fasana nahi hota,
is zamaane mein har shakhs sukoon se zinda nahi hota.

Meri daastaan sun'ne wala koi mila hi nahi umr bhar,
aur aaj meri qabr par har koi roya jaa raha hota.

04

Log sharaab ka nasha karte hain,
aur hum tere ishq mein behosh phirte hain.

Jaan-e-man, kya samjho gi meri deewangi ko,
hum toh tere naam par hi zinda phirte hain.

Kabhi yaad bhi kar liya karo humein,
ae mohtarma, hum roz teri yaadon mein thoda-thoda marte hain.

05

Wo khud bhi anjaan thi meri mohabbat se,
magar mujhe ishq mein deewana kar gayi.

Hazaar baar pukara uska naam maine,
na usne suna, na palat kar dekha kabhi.

Mohabbat bhi kya ajeeb shay hai janaab,
marte dam tak uski yaadon mein tadapte rahe.

Kaash woh ek baar sun leti meri khamoshi ko,
shayad keh paati kitni shiddat se chaha tha use.
