05 / RAAT

Thoughts
after midnight.

When the world falls silent, the thoughts we hide become louder.

01

Pyaar karna bhi ek gunaah ban gaya,
Magar mere dil ko bhi mujrim banne ka shauq tha.

Aashiq, tune aisa jaadu kiya,
Ki tere naam ke bina
Mera dil dhadakna hi bhool gaya.

Ab saza bhi teri,
Aur gunaah bhi tera naam…
Bas ek dil tha mera,
Jo tujhe chah kar
Khud hi mujrim ban gaya.

02

Tu chaand tha,
Aur main ek panchi…

Udna toh aata tha mujhe,
Bas itna nadaan tha ki
Teri roshni ko apna aasman samajh baitha.

Har baar tere paas aane ko uda,
Har baar thoda aur toot kar gira…

Aakhir samajh aaya—
Panchi chahe jitna bhi ud le,
Chaand tak kabhi nahi pahunchta.

Aur main…
Saari zindagi usi chaand ko
Apna samajh kar udta raha.

03

Aisi mayusi ban chuka hoon sabke liye,
Na aashiq humse khush hain, na hi maa-baap.

Pyaar toh sabko kar leta hoon,
Bas khud se pyaar karna hi bhool chuka hoon.

Tamaam baadalon ko garajte dekhta hoon,
Aur phir bhi baarish ka intezaar karta hoon—
Shayad kisi din ye aasman
Meri khamoshi ko samajh kar baras pade.
