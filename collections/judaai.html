03 / JUDAAI

Some
goodbyes stay.

Some people leave, but their absence never does.

01

Juda hokar bhi tumse juda ho nahi paate,
Har raat tumhari yaadon se bach nahi paate.

Socha tha waqt ke saath sab bhool jayenge,
Magar waqt guzarta gaya, aur hum tumhe aur chahte gaye.

02

Tum chale gaye toh laga sab khatam ho gaya,
Dil toh dhadakta raha, magar jeena kam ho gaya.

Ab kisi se mohabbat karne ka hausla nahi,
Ek tumhare jaane se hi ishq se bharosa uth gaya.

03

Teri judai mein humne khud ko tanha paaya hai,
Har khushi ke peeche tera gham chhupaya hai.

Log kehte hain waqt sab dard mita deta hai,
Shayad unhone kabhi kisi apne ko khoya nahi hai.

04

Tum paas nahi ho, phir bhi har pal paas lagti ho,
Meri khamoshi mein bhi tumhari awaaz lagti ho.

Kaise kahun tumse ki kitna toot chuka hoon main,
Tumhari yaad aati hai toh saans lena bhi saza lagti hai.

05

Humse bichhad kar tum khush reh lena,
Meri kami ko kisi aur se poora kar lena.

Bas ek baar mudkar dekh lena meri taraf,
Shayad meri aankhon mein tumhe apna hi ishq nazar aa jaaye.

06

Judaai ne humse woh sab cheen liya,
Jo tumhare saath rehkar kabhi khone ka darr tha.

Ab na tum ho, na woh pehle jaisa dil,
Bas tumhari yaadein hain aur ek tanha sa safar.
