05 / KHAMOSHI

Famous
Khamoshi.

Sometimes silence says what words were never brave enough to say.

01

Meri khamoshiyon mein larzan hai,
mere naalon ki gum-shuda aawaaz.

— Faiz Ahmad Faiz
02

Mustaqil bolta hi rehta hoon,
kitna khamosh hoon main andar se.

Mere andar ki khamoshi ko kaun samjhe,
main jo kehna chahta hoon keh nahi paata.

— Jaun Eliya
03

Harf-e-taaza nai khushbu mein likha chahta hai,
baab ik aur mohabbat ka khula chahta hai.

Yahi khamoshi kai rang mein zaahir hogi,
aur kuchh roz ki woh shokh khula chahta hai.

— Parveen Shakir
04

Chup chup kyun rahte ho 'Nasir',
ye kya rog laga rakkha hai.

Tanhai mein baith ke aksar,
dil se kis ka gila karte ho?

— Nasir Kazmi
05

Aasmaan hai khamoshi-e-jaaved,
main bhi ab lab nahin hilaane ka.

Jo kehna tha keh diya dil ne,
ab kisi lafz ka sahaara nahin.

— Jaun Eliya
06

Use bechain kar jaaunga main bhi,
khamoshi se guzar jaaunga main bhi.

Na koi shikwa, na koi sawaal, bas chup rahunga,
aur ek din uski duniya se utar jaaunga main bhi.

— Ameer Qazalbash
07

Galat baaton ko khamoshi se sunna, haami bhar lena,
bahut hain faaide is mein magar achchha nahin lagta.

Jo sach kehne ki aadat ho, woh chup kaise rahe aakhir,
zubaan ko rok lena bhi kabhi achchha nahin lagta.

— Javed Akhtar
08

Raat ko maan liya dil ne muqaddar lekin,
raat ke haath pe ab koi diya chahta hai.

Teri khamoshi mein bhi ek awaaz hai shayad,
jo mere dil ko ab tak sunaai deti hai.

— Parveen Shakir
09

Khamoshi se adaa ho rasm-e-doori,
koi hangaama barpa kyun karein hum.

Juda hona agar taqdeer hi tha,
to phir shikva kisi se kyun karein hum?

— Jaun Eliya
10

Aaj dekha hai tujh ko der ke baad,
dil ki duniya mein phir ujala hai.

Khamoshi se teri yaad aayi,
aur phir dil bahut akela hai.

— Nasir Kazmi
