03 / JUDAAI

Famous
Judaai.

The distance between two hearts that never really learned to forget.

01

Us ke kuche se jo uTh ahl-e-wafa jaate hain,
Ta-nazar kaam kare ru-ba-qafa jaate hain.

Muttasil rote hi rahiye to bujhe aatish-e-dil,
Ek-do aansu to aur aag laga jaate hain.

Ek bimaar-e-judai hoon main, aap hi tis par,
Poochhne wale juda jaan ko kha jaate hain.

— Meer Taqi Meer
02

Tujh se qismat mein meri surat-e-qufl-e-abjad
Tha likha baat ke bante hi juda ho jaana.

Hai ye aalam ki bahut der se tanha hoon main,
Jaane kis shakhs ko dhoondhta hai mera dil har shaam.

— Mirza Ghalib
03

'Daagh' us bazm mein mehman kahan jaata hai,
Tera Allah nigahban kahan jaata hai.

Hijr ke din ki musibat to guzar jaayegi,
Vasl ki raat ka ehsaan kahan jaata hai.

Rooth kar bazm se uTTha to na roka mujh ko,
Na kaha us ne, kaha maan kahan jaata hai.

— Daagh Dehlvi
04

Haal-e-dil yaar ko likhun kyunkar,
Haath dil se juda nahin hota.

Kis qadar dard-e-ishq hai dil mein,
Ek lamha bhi juda nahin hota.

Tum jo milte ho to lagta hai mujhe, waqt thehar gaya,
Tum jo jaate ho to dil se kuchh juda nahin hota.

— Momin Khan Momin
05

Bani thi kuchh ik us se muddat ke baad,
So phir bigdi pahli hi sohbat ke baad.

Judaai ke haalaat mein kya kahun,
Qayamat thi ek ek saa'at ke baad.

Nazar 'Meer' ne kaisi hasrat se ki,
Bahut roye hum us ki rukhsat ke baad.

— Meer Taqi Meer
06

Lagta nahin hai dil mera ujDe dayaar mein,
Kis ki bani hai aalam-e-na-paaidaar mein.

Keh do in hasraton se kahin aur ja basen,
Itni jagah kahan hai dil-e-daagh-daar mein.

Ek shaakh-e-gul pe baiTh ke bulbul ne kuchh kaha,
Dil ka fasana chhup na saka us ke izhaar mein.

— Bahadur Shah Zafar
07

Jite-ji koocha-e-dildaar se jaaya na gaya,
Us ki deewar ka sar se mere saaya na gaya.

Aatish-e-tez judaai mein yakayak us bin,
Dil jala yun ki tanik ji bhi jalaya na gaya.

Khaak tak koocha-e-dildaar ki chhaani hum ne,
Justuju ki pe dil-e-gum-shuda paaya na gaya.

— Meer Taqi Meer
08

Aap ka e'tibaar kaun kare,
Roz ka intezaar kaun kare.

Hijr mein zahr kha ke mar jaaun,
Maut ka intezaar kaun kare.

Waada karte nahin, ye kehte hain,
Tujh ko ummeedwaar kaun kare.

— Daagh Dehlvi
09

Ye na thi hamari qismat ki visaal-e-yaar hota,
Agar aur jeete rehte yahi intezaar hota.

Tere waade par jiye hum to ye jaan jhooT jaana,
Ki khushi se mar na jaate agar e'tibaar hota.

Koi mere dil se poochhe tere teer-e-neemkash ko,
Ye khalish kahan se hoti jo jigar ke paar hota.

— Mirza Ghalib
10

Tab-e-dil sarf-e-judai ho chuki,
Ya'ni taaqat aazmaai ho chuki.

ChhuTta kab hai asiir-e-khush-zabaan,
Jiite-ji apni rihaai ho chuki.

Biich mein hum hi na hon to lutf kya,
Rahm kar ab bewafai ho chuki.

— Meer Taqi Meer
