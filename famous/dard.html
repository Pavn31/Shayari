02 / DARD

Famous
Dard.

Some wounds become words, and some words never stop hurting.

01

Dil hi to hai na sang-o-khisht, dard se bhar na aaye kyun?
Royenge hum hazaar baar, koi hamein sataaye kyun?

Qaid-e-hayaat o band-e-gham, asl mein dono ek hain,
Maut se pehle aadmi, gham se nijaat paaye kyun?

— Mirza Ghalib
02

Dard-o-andoh mein thehra jo raha, main hi hoon,
Rang-o-ru jis ke kabhi munh na chadha, main hi hoon.

Jis pe karte ho sada jaur-o-jafa, main hi hoon,
Phir bhi jis ko hai gumaan tum se wafa mein hi hoon.

— Meer Taqi Meer
03

Ye qissa wo nahin tum jis ko qissa-khwan se suno,
Mere fasana-e-gham ko meri zaban se suno.

Sunao dard-e-dil apna to dam-ba-dam fariyad,
Misaal-e-nai meri har ek ustukhwan se suno.

— Bahadur Shah Zafar
04

Qahr hai maut hai qaza hai ishq,
sach to yun hai buri bala hai ishq.

Asar-e-gham zara bata dena,
wo bahut poochhte hain kya hai ishq.

Aafat-e-jaan hai koi parda-nashin,
ki mere dil mein aa chhupa hai ishq.

— Momin Khan Momin
05

Hai turfa tamasha sar-e-bazaar-e-mohabbat,
Sar bechte phirte hain kharidaar-e-mohabbat.

Allah kare tu bhi ho bimaar-e-mohabbat,
Sadqe mein chhute tere giraftaar-e-mohabbat.

Is waste dete hain wo har roz naya daagh,
Ek dard ke khugar na hon bimaar-e-mohabbat.

— Daagh Dehlvi
06

Ai zabt dekh ishq ki un ko khabar na ho,
Dil mein hazaar dard uthe aankh tar na ho.

Muddat mein shaam-e-vasl hui hai mujhe naseeb,
Do-chaar saal tak to ilahi sahar na ho.

Taal-e-shab-e-visaal ho misl-e-shab-e-firaaq,
Nikle na aaftab ilahi sahar na ho.

— Ameer Minai
07

Dard to maujood hai dil mein dawa ho ya na ho,
Bandagi haalat se zaahir hai khuda ho ya na ho.

Jhume hai shaakh-e-gul, khilte hain ghunche dam-ba-dam,
Ba-asar gulshan mein tahrik-e-saba ho ya na ho.

— Akbar Allahabadi
08

Kis shaam se utha tha mere dil mein dard sa,
So ho chala hoon peshtar-az-subh sard sa.

Baitha hoon jun ghubaar-e-zaeef, ab wagarna main,
Phirta raha hoon galiyon mein aawara-gard sa.

— Meer Taqi Meer
09

Bhari hai dil mein jo hasrat, kahun to kis se kahun?
Sune hai kaun musibat, kahun to kis se kahun?

Na Kohkan hai na Majnun ki the mere hamdard,
Main apna dard-e-mohabbat kahun to kis se kahun?

— Bahadur Shah Zafar
10

Main ro ke aah karunga jahan rahe na rahe,
Zameen rahe na rahe aasman rahe na rahe.

Rahe wo jaan-e-jahan ye jahan rahe na rahe,
Makeen ki khair ho ya rab makan rahe na rahe.

Chala to hoon pae-izhaar-e-dard-e-dil dekhun,
Huzoor-e-yaar majaal-e-bayaan rahe na rahe.

— Ameer Minai
