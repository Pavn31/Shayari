04 / ZINDAGI

Famous
Zindagi.

Life, in all its beauty, chaos, silence and unfinished stories.

01

Zindagi apni jab is shakl se guzri 'Ghalib',
Hum bhi kya yaad karenge ki khuda rakhte the.

Aur to rakhne ko hum dahr mein kya rakhte the,
Faqat ek sher mein andaaz-e-rasa rakhte the.

Us ka ye haal ki koi na ada-sanj mila,
Aap likhte the hum aur aap uTha rakhte the.

— Mirza Ghalib
02

Har nafas umr-e-guzishta ki hai mayyat 'Fani',
Zindagi naam hai mar mar ke jiye jaane ka.

Aah ko chaahiye ek umr asar hone tak,
Kaun jeeta hai teri zulf ke sar hone tak.

— Fani Badayuni
03

Jaan hum tujh pe diya karte hain,
Naam tera hi liya karte hain.

Chaak karne ke liye ai naseh,
Hum gareban siya karte hain.

Saghar-e-chashm se hum baada-parast,
Mai-e-didaar piya karte hain.

Zindagi zinda-dili ka hai naam,
Murda-dil khaak jiya karte hain.

— Imam Bakhsh Nasikh
04

Misal-e-saya mohabbat mein jaal apna hoon,
Tumhare saath giraftaar-e-haal apna hoon.

Meri numood ne mujh ko kiya barabar khaak,
Main naqsh-e-pa ki tarah paemal apna hoon.

Hui hai zindagi dushwar, mushkil aasaan kar,
Phirun chalun to hoon par main wabaal apna hoon.

— Meer Taqi Meer
05

Lagta nahin hai dil mera ujDe dayaar mein,
Kis ki bani hai aalam-e-na-paaidaar mein.

Keh do in hasraton se kahin aur ja basen,
Itni jagah kahan hai dil-e-daagh-daar mein.

Umr-e-daraaz maang kar laaye the chaar din,
Do aarzu mein kaT gaye, do intezaar mein.

— Bahadur Shah Zafar
06

Ik muamma hai samajhne ka na samjhane ka,
Zindagi kaahe ko hai khwaab hai deewane ka.

Zindagi kya hai anasir mein zuhoor-e-tarteeb,
Maut kya hai inhin ajza ka pareshan hona.

Har taraf ek tamasha hai magar hum khud bhi,
Ek kirdaar hain is parde pe nazar aane ka.

— Fani Badayuni
07

Zindagi zinda-dili ka hai naam,
Murda-dil khaak jiya karte hain.

Sang-e-asvad bhi hai bhaari patthar,
Log jo choom liya karte hain.

Kal na dega koi miTTi bhi unhein,
Aaj zar jo ki diya karte hain.

— Imam Bakhsh Nasikh
08

Bazicha-e-atfaal hai duniya mere aage,
Hota hai shab-o-roz tamasha mere aage.

Ek khel hai aurang-e-sulaiman mere nazdeek,
Ek baat hai ijaaz-e-masihaa mere aage.

Hota hai nihayat jo koi bhi mushkil,
Aasaan nazar aata hai duniya mere aage.

— Mirza Ghalib
09

Na-umeedi maut se kehti hai apna kaam kar,
Aas kehti hai Thahar, khat ka jawaab aane ko hai.

Zindagi ki raah mein har mod par milta raha,
Koi gham aane ko tha, koi gham jaane ko hai.

— Fani Badayuni
10

Ujale apni yaadon ke hamare saath rehne do,
Na jaane kis gali mein zindagi ki shaam ho jaaye.

Ye jo kuchh log hain mere safar mein, saath chalne do,
Na jaane kis maqam par ye sab bichhad jaayein.

— Bashir Badr
