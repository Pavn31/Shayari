Famous
Ishq.

Words of love that survived the people who wrote them.

01

Ishq par zor nahin hai ye wo aatish 'Ghalib',
ki lagaaye na lage aur bujhaaye na bane.

Maut ki raah na dekhun ki bin aaye na rahe,
tum ko chaahun ki na aao to bulaaye na bane.

— Mirza Ghalib
02

Kya kahun tum se main ki kya hai ishq,
jaan ka rog hai, bala hai ishq.

Ishq hi ishq hai jahan dekho,
saare aalam mein bhar raha hai ishq.

— Meer Taqi Meer
03

Ishq ko be-naqaab hona tha,
aap apna jawaab hona tha.

Teri aankhon ka kuchh qusoor nahin,
haan mujhi ko kharaab hona tha.

— Jigar Moradabadi
04

Taban jo noor-e-husn ba-seema-e-ishq hai,
kis darja dil-pazir tamasha-e-ishq hai.

Main kya hoon ek zarra-e-sehra-e-ishtiyaq,
dil kya hai ek qatra-e-dariya-e-ishq hai.

— Hasrat Mohani
05

Hai turfa tamasha sar-e-bazaar-e-mohabbat,
sar bechte phirte hain kharidaar-e-mohabbat.

Allah kare tu bhi ho bimaar-e-mohabbat,
sadqe mein chhute tere giraftaar-e-mohabbat.

— Daagh Dehlvi
06

Ishq mein la-jawaab hain hum log,
mahtaab-o-aftaab hain hum log.

Garche ahl-e-sharaab hain hum log,
ye na samjho kharaab hain hum log.

— Jigar Moradabadi
07

Rah-e-door-e-ishq mein rota hai kya,
aage aage dekhiye hota hai kya.

Ye nishaan-e-ishq hain jaate nahin,
daagh chhaati ke abas dhota hai kya.

— Meer Taqi Meer
08

Dard-e-dil ki unhen khabar na hui,
koi tadbeer kaargar na hui.

Koshishein hum ne ki hazaar magar,
ishq mein ek mo'tabar na hui.

— Hasrat Mohani
09

Ishq mein ne khauf-o-khatar chahiye,
jaan ke dene ko jigar chahiye.

Qabil-e-aaghosh sitam-didgaan,
ashk sa paakiza guhar chahiye.

— Meer Taqi Meer
10

Is ishq ke haathon se hargiz na mafar dekha,
utni hi badhi hasrat jitna hi udhar dekha.

Tha khel sa pahle ishq, lekin jo khulin aankhen,
dooba hua rag-rag mein vo teer-e-nazar dekha.

— Jigar Moradabadi
